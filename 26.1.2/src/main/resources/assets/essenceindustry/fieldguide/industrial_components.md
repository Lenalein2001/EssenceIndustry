# Industrial components

Two reusable parts connect the nuclear machinery recipes. Both are made in an ordinary crafting table, before building a reactor or fuel plant. They do not require AE2 or nuclear fuel.

## Industrial Control Circuit

One craft makes **four circuits** from four Copper Ingots, two Iron Ingots, two Redstone Dust and one Nether Quartz. Circuits provide the controls for reactor and turbine controllers, fuel-plant controllers, fuel actuators and fabrication heads.

## Induction Winding

One craft makes **two windings** from four Copper Ingots, one Iron Ingot and one Redstone Dust. Windings provide the electromagnetic parts for Energy Ports, turbine controllers and bearings, fuel actuators and centrifuges.

## What to make first

For a starter reactor with one fuel column, craft **one circuit batch and two winding batches**. This supplies the components for one Reactor Controller, one Energy Port and one Fuel Actuator, with one circuit and one winding left over. You still need the other ingredients shown in each part's recipe.

The three fuel-plant controllers each use one circuit. Their center ingredients identify the process: a Blast Furnace for enrichment, a Cauldron for reprocessing and a Piston for fabrication.

These are ordinary machine components. Quantum Alloy, Quantum Circuits and Quantum Cores remain the separate, more expensive branch used by quantum tools and their modules. The Model Copier also consumes one Quantum Circuit per copy.
